Soul Weather Privacy Policy

SoulRealm Studios

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Soul Weather is an Android weather app from SoulRealm Studios. This policy explains what the app does with information on a phone.

Soul Weather is intended for a general audience. It is not specifically directed at children under 13.

You can use Soul Weather without creating an account. SoulRealm Studios does not run a server that stores your searches, location, or ad requests. The app does not include a separate analytics product.

Location

Location permission is optional. If you tap Use My Location, Android asks for approximate location only. The app sends that approximate location to Visual Crossing to find the nearby place and load its forecast. The app does not request precise location and does not track your location in the background. If you deny permission, you can search for a city instead.

Weather requests

When you search, the place name you type is sent from your phone to Visual Crossing, which returns the closest matching place and its forecast. When a saved city's forecast loads, that city's coordinates are sent from your phone to Visual Crossing. Visual Crossing is an independent service, and its own terms apply to those requests.

What stays on the phone

The app saves these choices on the phone:

  • Saved cities, including name, region, country, and coordinates
  • The default city and the city you last viewed
  • Celsius or Fahrenheit, and km/h or mph
  • The selected Realm
  • Whether reduce animations is on
  • Whether first-time setup is finished

SoulRealm Studios does not upload that saved information. Android backup is turned on for the app, and no backup exclusion is set. If Android backup is turned on for the phone, Android may include this app's local data in a cloud backup. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes that data from the phone. A cloud backup may still keep it after you uninstall the app, and Android may restore it if you install Soul Weather again.

Advertising

Soul Weather shows one banner at the bottom of the weather screen through Google AdMob. During development, the app uses Google's test banner ads, which are labeled as test ads. Released versions may show real Google AdMob banner ads.

The ad request made by Soul Weather does not include your city name or coordinates. Google AdMob and Google's consent tool are part of the app. Google describes the information its advertising services may receive, including an advertising ID, IP address, and information about interactions with an ad, at the pages linked below.

Each time the app opens, it asks Google's User Messaging Platform whether a consent choice is required. If Google requires a form, Soul Weather shows that form before requesting an ad. The app requests an ad only when Google's tool says an ad may be requested. Google's ad software uses that choice. If the choice allows an ad but does not allow a personalized ad, Google may show a non-personalized ad instead. If Google's tool says an ad may not be requested, Soul Weather does not request one.

Where Google requires a way to change this choice, Settings includes Ad privacy. That control opens Google's privacy options form.

The consent request does not mark the user as under the age of consent. This policy does not describe AdMob account settings outside the app.
